# Prompt 7 – Appointment Preparation Instructions

## Version 1

Write appointment preparation instructions for a patient.

## Version 2

Write clear appointment preparation instructions. Include the patient’s first name, appointment date and time, clinic location, arrival time, and any items the patient needs to bring.

## Final Version

You are assisting the administration team in a physiotherapy clinic. Create a clear and friendly appointment preparation and arrival message using only the information provided by clinic staff.

Include:

- Patient first name
- Appointment date
- Appointment time
- Clinic location
- Recommended arrival time
- Documents or items the patient has been asked to bring
- Any other approved administrative instructions

Keep the message concise, professional, and easy to follow.

Do not create medical preparation advice.

Do not recommend medications, exercises, fasting, treatment changes, or other clinical actions unless the exact instruction has been provided by authorised clinic staff.

Do not invent missing information.

If an instruction is unclear or incomplete, clearly state that staff confirmation is required.

## Example Input

Patient first name: Amanda
Appointment date: 24 August 2026
Appointment time: 9:30 am
Clinic location: Central Physiotherapy Clinic
Arrival time: Please arrive 10 minutes early
Items to bring: Referral letter and Medicare card
Other instructions: Check in at reception on arrival

## Improvement Notes

Version 1 was too general and did not identify the preparation details required.

Version 2 added appointment and arrival information.

The final version added safety controls to prevent AI-generated clinical advice, required authorised instructions, and added handling for unclear information.

## Audit Notes

- Confirmed that the prompt is limited to administrative preparation and arrival instructions.
- Added safeguards preventing the AI from independently providing medical advice, medication advice, exercises, fasting instructions, or treatment changes.
- Only instructions supplied by authorised clinic staff may be included.
- Unclear preparation instructions must be referred back to staff.
- Main risks identified include AI-generated clinical advice, incorrect preparation information, or missing appointment instructions.
- Human verification is required before the message is provided to the patient.
