# Prompt 4 – GP Referral or Document Request

## Version 1

Write an email asking a GP clinic for patient documents.

## Version 2

Write a professional email to a GP clinic requesting patient documents. Include the patient’s name, date of birth, requested documents, reason for the request, and clinic contact details.

## Final Version

You are assisting the administration team in a physiotherapy clinic. Create a concise and professional email to a GP clinic or medical practice requesting specified patient documents using only the information provided.

Include:

- GP clinic or recipient name, if provided
- Patient full name
- Patient date of birth, if required for identification
- Documents being requested
- Administrative reason for the request, if provided
- A polite request for the documents to be forwarded
- Clinic contact details, if provided

Keep the email professional, respectful, and suitable for communication between healthcare organisations.

Only include the minimum patient information necessary.

Do not add diagnoses, treatment details, or medical history.

Do not request documents that have not been specified.

Do not invent patient or clinic information.

If important information is missing, clearly state that staff confirmation is required.

## Example Input

Recipient: Greenfield Medical Centre
Patient name: Olivia Brown
Date of birth: 12 March 1985
Documents requested: GP Management Plan and referral letter
Reason: Required for physiotherapy administration
Clinic contact: admin@centralphysio.com.au

## Improvement Notes

Version 1 did not specify the documents or patient identification details required.

Version 2 added the main administrative information.

The final version added privacy safeguards, minimum-data requirements, restrictions on clinical information, and clear handling of missing information.

## Audit Notes

- Checked that only the minimum patient identification information is requested.
- Added restrictions preventing the AI from adding diagnoses, medical history, or treatment information.
- Confirmed that the AI cannot request documents that were not specifically authorised.
- Missing patient or document information must be confirmed by staff.
- Staff should verify the recipient before sending patient information.
- Main risks identified include privacy breaches, incorrect recipient details, excessive disclosure, or requesting the wrong documents.
- Human review is required before external healthcare communication is sent.
